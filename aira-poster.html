Aira: A Smart Multi-Modal Flashcard Ecosystem for Children with Multiple Disabilities

Iniyan V | B.E. CSE (AI & ML) | Rajalakshmi Engineering College | In collaboration with NIEPMD
1

Problem Statement

Children with multiple disabilities — including autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disability and communication disorders — benefit from repeated, multi-sensory vocabulary instruction. Printed flashcards, however, stop at a single picture and a single word. They cannot show how a concept relates to its group, use, action, properties, place or associations, and they cannot speak the child's home language.

Therapists and parents also lack a lightweight way to author personalised, image-rich card sets and keep them consistent across home and classroom.

3

Proposed Solution

Aira turns one printed trigger image into a six-concept learning moment — authored once, opened by camera, narrated in four languages.

01

Scan the card

The Flutter app recognises a printed card with OpenCV ORB features and colour histograms. No QR code, no menu, no typing.

02

Explore six concepts

Group, Use, Action, Properties, Location and Association unfold as image + simple text around the trigger picture.

03

Listen in four languages

gTTS generates narration on demand in English, Tamil, Hindi and Malayalam — text and voice together.

Accessibility & Inclusion

  • Camera-first start — no reading, typing or menu needed to begin.
  • Large touch targets and consistent icon placement across screens.
  • Text + audio together — narration in English, Tamil, Hindi and Malayalam.
  • Low-distraction layouts — one idea per screen, clear hierarchy.
  • Low-cost Android phones supported, with a web fallback.
  • No specialist hardware — the phone camera is the only sensor.
  • Therapist-authored content — personalisation without engineering.
Large type · high contrast · low distraction
8

Impact & Novelty

Impact
  • One card becomes a network of six ideas, not one label.
  • Personalised decks can be authored in minutes and reused.
  • Multilingual by design — the home language is a first-class layer.
Novelty
  • 7-image card model: one trigger + six concept attributes.
  • Printed-card recognition without QR codes, using ORB features.
  • Unified admin + mobile API with on-demand TTS in four languages.

Future Scope
